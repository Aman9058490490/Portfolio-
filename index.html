Research

OptiRSDE — MIDAS Lab, IIIT-Delhi

Postgraduate Researcher | Dec 2024 - May 2026

Supervisor: Dr. Rajiv Ratn Shah | Delhi, India

Worked on OptiRSDE, a real-time stereo depth estimation pipeline for robotics, autonomous driving, and industrial safety. The system integrates optimized feature matching, QR-based synchronization, and temporal smoothing to deliver <3% error at 50m and near 7.5% at 100m while running 10-20x faster than conventional methods.

Tech Stack: Python, OpenCV, PyTorch, YOLOv8, AMD Ryzen 5 4600H CPU, NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650 Ti GPU, dual 4K cameras, distance-measuring device.
